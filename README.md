==================================================

ABOUT CONVERT STUDIO

==================================================

Convert Studio is a digital studio that creates websites and digital systems for small businesses.

The website itself is an important demonstration of the quality of the work we sell.

Most visitors will arrive from Instagram.

Instagram already introduces and sells the idea behind our products.

Therefore, this website does NOT need to be a long traditional landing page.

Its job is to:

1. establish trust

2. present Convert Studio professionally

3. let visitors explore real demos

4. organize our offers

5. make pricing easier to understand

6. make contacting us simple

Think of this website as a:

COMPACT COMMERCIAL SHOWCASE

rather than:

- a traditional agency landing page

- a SaaS dashboard

- a Linktree clone

- a corporate website

- a long sales funnel

The experience should be quick to explore and require very little effort from the visitor.

==================================================

CORE BRAND FEELING

==================================================

The website should communicate:

“Modern digital solutions made simple.”

Convert Studio should feel:

professional without being distant

premium without looking expensive

technological without looking complicated

friendly without looking childish

minimal without looking empty

commercial without feeling aggressive

Avoid technical jargon in the interface.

The visitor should quickly think:

“They know what they're doing.”

“I understand what they offer.”

“I can actually see what they build.”

“This looks professional.”

“It seems easy to hire them.”

==================================================

VISUAL DIRECTION

==================================================

The core Convert Studio identity is:

PURPLE

BLACK / CHARCOAL

WHITE / WARM OFF-WHITE

Create a sophisticated LIGHT interface.

Do NOT make the website predominantly dark.

At the same time, avoid the opposite problem:

do NOT create an overly white, washed-out, sterile interface.

Use visual layering.

Suggested direction:

Page background:

warm off-white / subtle lavender-gray

Main surfaces:

soft warm white

Cards:

clean white or slightly contrasting neutral surfaces

Primary typography:

near-black / charcoal

Brand:

rich Convert Studio purple

Strategic high-contrast elements:

charcoal / near-black

Use purple intentionally, not everywhere.

Purple should communicate the brand through:

- brand mark

- labels

- interactions

- arrows

- active states

- subtle borders

- small highlights

- CTA details

Use black/charcoal to give the design confidence and prevent the interface from becoming overly soft.

The visual balance should roughly feel like:

60% warm light neutrals

25% charcoal / strong neutral contrast

15% purple / brand accents

This is a conceptual balance, not a literal pixel requirement.
